**The standing argument** is every premise whose failure would reopen the decision: the goal it
derives a constraint from, as a reference with that constraint stated in one sentence beside it,
and a decision of another Component it depends on, as a reference; the
measurement it rests on, with the command that takes it again, one a reader can run on the corpus
it names: a script that no tree holds is replaced by such a command, or kept in the message of the
commit that took it, which the head names by its subject; the fact that defeated its nearest
rival. A constraint from a goal binds outright, where one from a decision is a presumption, and the
reference is what tells a reader which. **The test: if this premise turned false, would the
decision have to be argued again? If yes, it is in the head.** If no, it is deliberation.

%% The table: `design@agent-skills@heads-stand-on-argument`. The weak form and its guard:
%% `design@agent-skills@fork-choice-in-weak-form`; whose ranking a fork's choice names:
%% `design@agent-skills@fork-choice-names-whose-ranking-it-was`.
**A head stands on its argument.** Most decisions are proposed, argued over their costs and rivals,
and approved by the owner. The head records such a decision on that argument, with its costs and
rivals in its body or in the `rejected` entries it cites. What the owner brings lands by its kind:

| what the owner brings | where it lands |
| --- | --- |
| a fact only the owner holds, a doubt included | a premise, attributed as the owner's report and open to challenge |
| a judgement of what is plausible or of expected use | a premise, attributed as the owner's report and open to challenge |
| an intent that states a purpose of the project, a scope that bounds one or a ranking between two, as `skill@knowledge-architect-goal-setting@goal-or-not` tests them, and that no goal and no recorded decision already serves against every rival it is cited to defeat | a goal, drafted under `skill@knowledge-architect-goal-setting`, which the head cites |
| an argument, one that weighs a cost included | argued, and recorded on its merits |
| an approval of the session's default, threshold or weighing, whatever its words ("approved", "agreed", "all defaults approved", "accepted the cost") | no ground: the session's decision, kept in a plan document's items and the commit message; at a fork, the session's ranking, in the weak form below |
| a reading of a source | the source |
| a choice the argument leaves open, made by the owner: a pick at a fork against the session's default or with none offered, a threshold the owner sets, a local cost the owner accepts | first, ask whether the choice shows a ranking between two purposes that holds across decisions, which is then a goal to draft; otherwise, the weak form: "X over Y: the owner ranked <cost> above <cost>, which no goal ranks" |

A preference about a mechanism, or an intent that is none of those three, is no case of its own:
it is an argument, or a choice at a fork.

**The weak form.** A fork's choice names the rivals, the costs weighed, and whose ranking it was,
and reopens without the owner on an argument or a fact that ranks the shapes,
since the question is then no longer a fork. An argument or a fact that the fork's record
already holds reopens nothing: this guards a choice from reversals back and forth.

**A head whose ground cites the owner's words is read by the same table**: a ground that cites the owner's pick
at a fork is a fork's choice in the weak form, an approval cited as a ground is no ground, a ground
that cites the owner's argument is an argument, recorded on its merits, and an intent that states a
purpose, a scope or a ranking is a goal to draft.

%% Provenance routes a defeated premise, per `primer@owner-word-premise`, and a new member, per

**A design head, a `rejected` entry, a tripwire and an issue entry name no one as the source of an
argument or of an approval.** They attribute, to the owner or to the session, only what a session
could not derive from inside the project, from its goals, its record, its code, its measurements
and the sources it follows, and attribute it to where it first came from, not to who approved it:

- a fact, and a judgement of what is plausible or of expected use: "the owner reported", or "the
  session assumed";
- the ranking of a cost no goal ranks, which a fork's choice, a threshold set and a local cost
  accepted are: "X over Y: the owner ranked <cost> above <cost>, which no goal ranks", or "the
  session ranked"; a ranking of the session that the owner approved stays the session's;
- a choice among the readings a source permits: "the owner chose among the readings the source
  permits";
- an act a goal reserves to the owner: "on the owner's word".

Where the record of a fork names no costs, a
conversion of an older attribution writes "X over Y, which no goal ranks: the owner's ranking", or
"the session's", after reading the head's history, `git log -L`, and the plan document that carried
the fork, and never costs it has to invent. A plan document's items and a commit message attribute
the owner's rulings and arguments.
None of these texts quotes the owner: the verbatim words of a design discussion stay in its plan
document's committed transcripts, and in a committed file that leaves once its outcomes are carried
out. An attribution keeps the scope and the hedges of the owner's words, and is checked, like a
quotation, against the question it answered.

%% `design@agent-skills@owner-words-kept-in-an-issue-on-request`.
An issue entry holds the owner's words verbatim only where the owner asks it to keep them, marked
as quoted on the owner's request, as input to a later discussion and never as the ground of a
ruling; the rest of the entry attributes.

**What the ground means to a later session.** A head that stands on its argument is reversed by a
better argument, which the owner approves where the reversal is a fork, per
`primer@question-authority`. A fork's choice reopens as the weak form says, through
`skill@knowledge-architect-design`, the reopening reported under "Decided". A premise the owner
reported gives way to a measurement, as `primer@owner-word-premise` says, and a goal changes only
through the owner.
