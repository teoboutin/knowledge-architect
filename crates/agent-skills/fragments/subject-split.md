A level-two subject of a design home holds the heads a piece of work reads together: one part of the
Component, such as a command, a register or an activity. The record search reads each subject's
heads together, with the `rejected` entries they cite and the issues and tripwires that cite them,
each of the same Component or declared location, as one cluster that one agent reads whole. A subject
is split into subjects read apart when:

- `{{command}} worklist --out <dir>` names its cluster in a notice, as its own entries exceed the
  bound the work leaves of the words one agent is given; or
- its heads answer questions about parts of the Component that change apart.

%% Planned before the harvest where planning finds it: `design@agent-skills@subject-split-standard`.
A split moves headings and decides nothing. One that the planning of a piece of work finds is a step
before the harvest that writes into the subject, as
`skill@knowledge-architect-planning@cutting-steps-and-slices` says; one met otherwise, outside planning
or while the work is built, is a fix in a commit of its own, as `primer@met-outside-the-task` says. To see the notices, run `{{command}} worklist --out <dir>` with
`<dir>` a new directory of your scratch space outside the project.
