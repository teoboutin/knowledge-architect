# A goal the record search graded was missed by the work in two separate pieces of work

## Summary

The tripwire that watched `design@agent-skills@searcher-grades-goals`, on a goal missed where the
searcher graded the goals, fired at its second instance and left its home, so
`design@agent-skills@searcher-grades-goals` and `design@agent-skills@seeds-name-decisions` are
reopened. In two plan documents, a goal the record search had graded `bears` or `may-bear`, and that
the work bears on, was found missing from the plan by its design-conformance review.

## Details

### What

- The first instance: the plan document of the goal standard listed as not read five goals the
  record search had graded `may-bear` and its work bears on, which its second design-conformance
  review found, in the commit "[review] Repairs of the goal-standard spec's second
  design-conformance review: step 6 keeps every Component with a goal, and a boundary block draws on
  the owner's statements where no work exists".
- The second: `goal@agent-skills@reviews-judge-by-the-writer-s-criteria`, graded `bears` by the
  search of the discussion of `milestone@plans@development-and-components` and recorded in that
  milestone's table of the entries graded `bears` with the outcome "constrains; read whole at
  grounding", was missing from the thread of the spec-first axis, which it constrains: the reviewer
  judges the code only against what binds its writer. The milestone's design-conformance review
  found it, and the commit "[review] Repairs of the plan documents' reviews: slice 1's sites of a
  fourth crate, the retrospective's two clauses, D1 and D2 awaiting the owner" lists it among the
  entries the plan's review found missing.

The suspected mechanism, in one sentence, `not established`: a goal graded `bears` or `may-bear` is
recorded with an outcome that names no thread or shape it constrains, so no shape is read against
it, and the search's grade stands in for the session's own judgement of each shape against the goal.

Re-entry point: the design audit of slice 4 of `milestone@plans@development-and-components`,
`spec@development-and-components@search-timing`, which changes when the design skill's record search
runs and lists this entry among its audit subjects.

### Why it matters

A goal binds outright, so a shape that contradicts a goal the session never judged it against
reaches the owner, or the tree, as if no goal bore on it; a design-conformance review found both
instances, after the plan document was written. It strains `design@agent-skills@searcher-grades-goals`,
whose premise is that the searcher's grades of the goals are a second reading beside the session's
own, and `design@agent-skills@seeds-name-decisions`, which seeds no goal.

### What would close it

A design discussion, at the re-entry point or on the owner's word, that keeps
`design@agent-skills@searcher-grades-goals` and `design@agent-skills@seeds-name-decisions` with the
reason, or changes how a goal the search grades reaches the session's judgement of each shape, such
as an outcome that names the shapes a graded goal constrains.
