# The search before the work missed standing entries the work bore on, in two pieces of work

## Summary

The standing-state reviews of two specs each reported open entries the work bore on that no search
before the work had returned. This fired the tripwire that guarded the premise of
`design@agent-skills@record-searched-before-the-work`: that the searches it places before
the work meet the standing entries a piece of work bears on. The decision is reopened on its
occasions.

## Details

### What

Instances seen, both on the main branch, in two separate pieces of work on the same day:

1. The work of the lint spec, the spec of the checker's lints on defined ids outside references.
   Its review commit "[review] Repairs of the last transcript review of the lint spec: the
   searched entries all have an outcome, the observations are named, the corrected arguments say
   so" records that the standing-state reviewer of that branch's spec reported two open entries the
   search before the work did not return: `issue@core@a-span-with-an-empty-head-is-malformed-against-its-head`
   and `issue@agent-skills@a-premortem-criterion-listed-as-a-default-reads-two-ways`.
2. The work of the spec of the `rejected` register. Its review commit "[review] Repairs of the
   review before the merge of the rejected register: heads split and cited, the owner's hedges
   kept, T1 narrowed to the case it watches" records that the spec's own standing-state review
   found three entries the discussion's search had not returned:
   `issue@core@a-plain-hash-item-name-is-read-by-nothing`,
   `tripwire@agent-skills@ruling-lost-in-assembly` and
   `tripwire@agent-skills@attribution-loses-the-scope`. Each named a rejected alternative in words.

The tripwire's clause asked for two such reports "across sessions". The history shows two separate
pieces of work, which is what the rule on slips of the tripwire standard asks of a recurrence.
Not established: that the two were separate sessions, since the history does not name sessions.
The first instance was recorded in its commit message only, and the tripwire's own list of
instances named the second alone.

Ruled out: the analysis of a thaum retrospective file on driven tests records the tripwire "not
met: a searcher returned the four entries, and the record omitted them". That concerns entries a
search did return, not the ones the lint spec's standing-state reviewer reported, and bears on
neither instance.

Every one of the five entries was recorded days before its discussion's search ran, dated with
`git log -S"##<slug>"` and `--diff-filter=A`, so an entry added after the search explains neither
instance. The misses were in the search itself: in how its agents derived their groups by hand, or
in how they judged an entry that named a rejected alternative in words.
`design@agent-skills@record-searched-before-the-work` now argues both instances, and the work list of
`design@core@worklist-writes-and-verifies-the-reports` takes the groups' assembly from the agents.

Re-entry point: the design discussion of
`issue@agent-skills@a-tripwire-on-agent-behaviour-fires-where-this-project-cannot-see`, on the
owner's word, given about the tripwire whose firing opened this entry: its reopening waits for that
discussion, whose plan is to use the retrospective skill to analyse other projects. That discussion
reopens `design@agent-skills@record-searched-before-the-work` on its occasions, with a
search when convergence is proposed, before the premortem, among the candidates.

### Why it matters

An entry the work bears on and that no search returned is met only at the review before the merge,
after the design has converged and the work is built, so a decision it should have changed is
built first. It strains `design@agent-skills@record-searched-before-the-work`, and
`goal@knowledge-architect@agents-work-without-drift`, which the head serves: work carried from
design to merge stays within the recorded design only while the record that bears on it reaches the
session before the design converges.

### What would close it

The discussion above, ending in a rewrite of
`design@agent-skills@record-searched-before-the-work` that moves or adds an occasion of
the search, or in that head keeping its occasions with the two instances argued in it, which it now
does; or the owner's word that this argument closes the entry without waiting for that discussion.
