# The application judge's reading of tripwires names no fact against the standing-state review as the catch

## Summary

`design@agent-skills@application-judge-reads-tripwires` answers one of the two rivals the closed
issue on the design-record cross-check named: the cross-check reading each tripwire clause by
clause before the application. It names no fact against the other: no change, the standing-state
review of the audit's branch being the catch. Its own evidence, from thaum, is that the
standing-state review found both tripwires the application left false.

## Details

### What

The issue on the design-record cross-check missing a tripwire that a split or a retitle leaves
false, closed by the commit "The design-record axis judges every entry the session's application changes, in a
second run of its workflow", named two rivals to a reading by the application's judge: a fix in the
cross-check, "clause by clause, against the head that now holds what each clause guards"; "or no
change, with the standing-state review of the audit's branch as the catch, which found both here".
The closed issue is read at the parent of that commit, under
`register@agent-skills@issue`.
The thread `#application-judge-reads-tripwires` in that commit's message argued against the
cross-check alone ("a fix in the cross-check runs before the application and can only predict its
edits"). The head, split out of `design@agent-skills@application-judged-by-diff` by the work on head titles
that must not mislead, carries that argument as it stood, and the head before the split had the
same gap. A design-record auditor briefed to check the split found it: the head fails "What a head
stands on", the fact that defeated its nearest rival, under the reading that the second rival is a
nearest rival; under the reading that only the rival the thread argued is, it conforms.

### Why it matters

A later session weighing the removal of the judge's reading of tripwires meets no argument against
leaving the catch to the standing-state review, and the thaum evidence the head cites, that review
finding both tripwires, reads as support for that rival.
`design@agent-skills@application-judge-reads-tripwires` is the decision it strains.

### What would close it

Either a sourced fact that ranks the judge's reading above the standing-state review as the catch,
such as a tripwire or an issue the application leaves false that the standing-state review does not
read, or a measurement of one, added to the head on the in-change path of
`skill@knowledge-architect-design`; or that design discussion concluding that the standing-state
review suffices, and the head reversed.
