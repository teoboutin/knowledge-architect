# Whether the texts that include one fragment count as separate sites for entry test 2

## Summary

Entry test 2 of `design@agent-skills@a-head-is-owed-by-an-entry-test` counts "each text that states
the instruction" as one site. A fragment of `path@agent-skills@fragments/` is one source that the
build copies into several installed texts. No record says whether those texts count as one site,
the fragment, or as one each, so whether a decision stated by a fragment alone earns a head by test
2 reads two ways.

## Details

### What

A design-record auditor checking the heads of the work on head titles that must not mislead raised it, at the review before the
merge of the commit "[plans] The spec on head titles that must not mislead leaves, its work landed
and its review before the merge read", on
the heads the shape fragment, `path@agent-skills@fragments/head-shape.md`, states: that fragment is
included in three installed texts, decision-recording, the decision-record reviewer and the
design-record auditor. The rule in `primer@where-knowledge-goes` that "A whole text that the
workflow's own build copies from one file into several installed texts is no restatement" bears on
it, since a copy is no restatement, but it does not say whether the copies are sites. The auditor
settled nothing.

### Why it matters

Read as one site, a decision a fragment alone states fails test 2 and its reason belongs beside
the fragment's text; read as one site per host, it passes. Each later audit of the design record
judges such heads one way or the other, against `goal@design-is-recorded-with-its-arguments`, and
two auditors can give opposite verdicts on the same head.

### What would close it

A reading recorded in `design@agent-skills@a-head-is-owed-by-an-entry-test`, under
`skill@knowledge-architect-design`, of whether a fragment's hosts are one site or several; this
entry is deleted in that commit.
