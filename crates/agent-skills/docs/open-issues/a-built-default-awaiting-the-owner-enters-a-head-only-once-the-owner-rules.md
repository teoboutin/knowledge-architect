# A built default awaiting the owner enters a design head only once the owner rules, where the owner wants agents more autonomous

## Summary

`skill@knowledge-architect-decision-recording@when-recording-happens` writes a default awaiting the
owner into a design head only once the owner rules on it. The owner wants that condition changed so
that agents are more autonomous about design, defaults and records. The owner's wish, recorded here
first, that more of the owner's intent move into goals is replaced, on the owner's word, by the
goal standard of `design@agent-skills@a-goal-is-a-purpose-the-project-exists-for`, under which a
goal is a purpose, a scope or a ranking and any other statement is design. The owner named two
opposite fixes, a default that enters the record with no ruling, and a default that needs the
owner's ruling stopping the work to ask, chose neither, and left the choice to this entry's
discussion.

## Details

### What

The owner's ruling at the retrospective intake of 2026-10-24, on a rootpoint session that worked
six slices of a milestone in one branch: a built default narrowed a head while it awaited the owner,
and the owner wants to change the condition "A default awaiting the owner is built as written … and
enters a design head once the owner rules on it". The analysis read the case against the planning
skill's sentence that "A scope change listed there does not start before its ruling", which the
session had not applied, and against
`issue@agent-skills@the-primer-s-divergence-paragraph-reads-two-ways-on-work-that-goes-on`, whose
question the case answers in real use.

The work that opened this entry answered the intake's first case: an approval of the
session's default at a fork is recorded as the session's choice, per
`design@agent-skills@fork-choice-in-weak-form`. Its third case, a milestone whose last slice landed
with defaults unruled, was answered before, by a rewording of the planning skill. It kept this condition, on the reading that the
owner's ruling decides when a head is written, not what it stands on; the owner has not ruled on
that reading.

Two further instances, in this repository, each a lapse of the condition, which is delivered at
both acts: `primer@installed-skills` loads `skill@knowledge-architect-decision-recording` before
writing into a design home, and the harvest of `skill@knowledge-architect-planning@working-a-slice`
loads it too.

- The pre-release fixes, 2026-11-01, on the in-change path: an in-change decision held a part put
  to the owner as a default; the session wrote it into the head in the implementing commit; the
  decision-record re-review found it, and it was removed and written back after the ruling: a head
  written, removed and written back in one session.
- The slice that built the `model` crate, 2026-11-04, a slice with no discussion: the content of
  one default, D4, was written into a head before the ruling, and the decision-record reviewer
  caught it.

The retrospective of the first proposed a pointer from the in-change path of
`skill@knowledge-architect-design@decision-authority` to
`skill@knowledge-architect-decision-recording@when-recording-happens`, +0 instructions. It was not
taken: the second instance is not on the in-change path, and the condition the pointer would
deliver again is the one the owner wants changed. These instances are evidence of the condition's
cost for the discussion. The analysis is W3 of
2026-11-01-knowledge-architect-pre-release-fixes-klarch-workflow, in the commit "[intake] The
analyses of forty received retrospective files, three outcomes for the owner".

Re-entry point: a design discussion under `skill@knowledge-architect-design`, on the owner's word.

### Why it matters

Every default held for the owner's word before it enters a head keeps a branch's record incomplete
until the ruling, against `goal@knowledge-architect@agents-work-without-drift`; a default written
into a head before the ruling records as settled what `goal@knowledge-architect@design-with-agents-as-counterparts`
asks the owner to meet before the merge.

### What would close it

A discussion under `skill@knowledge-architect-design` on when a built default awaiting the owner
enters a head, its outcome recorded in
`skill@knowledge-architect-decision-recording@when-recording-happens` and in the head that argues
it, or the owner's word that the condition stays as it is.
