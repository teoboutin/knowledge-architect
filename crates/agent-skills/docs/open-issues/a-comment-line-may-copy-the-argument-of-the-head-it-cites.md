# May a `%%` line of the installed text's source copy the argument of the head it cites?

## Summary

The root CLAUDE.md says the `%%` lines of a shipped text "carry the reasons and the citations of the
record". The primer's rule of one home per durable decision makes a second statement of a head's
argument a copy that drifts. Several `%%` lines on the main branch state a head's reason in full
beside the head's citation.

## Details

### What

The routing reviewer of slice 2 of the milestone that shipped the development skill reported five
`%%` lines of that branch that copied the argument of the head each cited; the branch cut them to
the pointer. The same pattern stands in older sources, such as the comment above the goal standard
in the goal-setting skill's source, which argues its rival in full.

### Why it matters

A head reworded later leaves the comment arguing the old reason, and nothing checks that the two
agree. It strains `design@agent-skills@shipped-text-line-comments` and the primer's
`primer@where-knowledge-goes`.

### What would close it

A reading of `design@agent-skills@shipped-text-line-comments` against the one-home rule: whether a
`%%` line is a reason recorded at the code, which the primer admits beside a head, or a copy; then
either the root CLAUDE.md's sentence narrowed to "the citations, and a reason no head holds", and the
existing copies cut, or the head stating why a comment may carry a head's reason.
