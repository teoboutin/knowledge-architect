# A head a change touches owes its whole conformance, older debts included, at any edit

## Summary

The rule that a touched head is brought to the rules on design heads in the change that touches it
reaches every head a change edits at all, and so every debt the head carried before the branch.
On a head that bundles decisions, one edited sentence or one removed citation has cost a split into
several heads, the re-pointing of every citing site and up to four re-review rounds, and twice the
rule was set aside by an issue entry. Each fix reverses part of a head, so it is a design question.

## Details

### What

The rule, in `path@agent-skills@fragments/head-shape.md`, which
`skill@knowledge-architect-decision-recording@head-shape` delivers: "**A head that a change touches
is brought to the rules on design heads before the change lands**: its title to the rule it argues,
each decision it bundles to a head of its own, its ground to its argument. In work a plan document
plans, a head its harvest will touch [...] is brought to them by a step before the harvest [...]; a
head the list missed, and a head any other change touches, in the change that touches it.
Re-pointing a reference in a head, to a renamed entry or a migrated form, does not touch it; adding
or removing one does." For a change no plan document plans, which every instance below was, the
rule acts at the edit. Its decision is `design@agent-skills@existing-heads-on-touch`, whose clause
on a removed reference is the owner's ruling.

Two entries already hold touched heads left unsplit:
`issue@knowledge-architect@the-git-flow-head-bundles-unargued-statements`, on the owner's word, and
`issue@check@core-heads-bundle-decisions-with-many-citing-sites`, whose heads were left unsplit
because a split must first read every site that cites the head, 6 to 39 sites per head.

The suspected mechanism, in one sentence: the rule assumed a touched head is one the change
rewrites, and it reaches every head a change edits at all, older debts included.

The shapes, each against `design@agent-skills@existing-heads-on-touch`:

1. keep the rule and accept the cost;
2. scope the touch to the statements the change writes, moves or makes false, each other failing
   statement opened as an issue entry naming the head in the same change, the issue triage's
   proposal; it bounds the cascade for every kind of edit, removal included, keeps re-review for
   what the branch wrote, and leaves older debts to the design-record audit, which
   `design@agent-skills@audit-repairs-the-whole-record` already makes the record's whole repair;
   it also narrows the touched-head lists of `design@agent-skills@scoped-auditor-on-touched-heads`
   and `design@agent-skills@pre-harvest-conformance`;
3. exempt a citation removed because its target left the record, as re-pointing is, the finding
   repairs' proposal; it narrows the owner's ruling on a removed reference, so it is put to the
   owner, per `primer@design-heads`, "The weak form".

Ruled out: a bound on re-review rounds, since every round found real defects. Bounds on any shape:
`design@agent-skills@one-decision-per-head` and `design@agent-skills@title-states-the-rule` stay,
only their moment changes; `design@agent-skills@term-defined-at-first-load` keeps the definition
of a touch in one text.

The proposals of shapes 2 and 3 argue from review rounds, so the discussion that decides them runs
the comparison of `issue@agent-skills@the-review-cost-counts-are-not-compared-yet`.

Re-entry point: the next change whose touched head owes the split of a decision older than the
branch, or the owner's word, for a discussion under `skill@knowledge-architect-design`.

### Why it matters

The premise of `design@agent-skills@existing-heads-on-touch`, that in a change no plan document
plans a head is repaired "at the cost of one read", fails on a bundled head: the cascade costs
re-review rounds for debts no branch wrote, against `goal@agents-get-a-complete-workflow`, and
twice the rule was set aside by an issue entry rather than followed, so the text no longer states
the practice that serves `goal@design-is-recorded-with-its-arguments`.

### What would close it

The discussion picks a shape, with the owner's word on the removal clause, and the fragment and
`design@agent-skills@existing-heads-on-touch` are rewritten to it, with the heads that read the
same definition; or it keeps the rule with its cost recorded in the head, and this entry is
deleted with that reason.
