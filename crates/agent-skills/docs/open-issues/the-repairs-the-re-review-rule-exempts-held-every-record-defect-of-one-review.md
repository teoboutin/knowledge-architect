# The repairs the re-review rule exempts held every record defect of one review

## Summary

In one review before a merge, the decision-record axis was sent again on a whole repair commit for
the two changes the re-review rule names, and found eight record defects, all in repairs the rule
exempts and none in the two it names. No defect was lost, since the axis read the whole commit. One
instance does not show that the exemption loses defects; this entry keeps it for a second.

## Details

### What

The rule, `skill@knowledge-architect-review@what-review-leaves`: "Any other repair, a rewording or
an argument, a mention or a reference added inside an existing head included, and a title brought
to what its head's body already argues, is reviewed by no axis again; one made before the last
transcript review is still read by it." Its decision is
`design@agent-skills@only-a-head-changing-repair-is-reviewed-again`.

The instance: the first slice of the goals' milestone in this repository, 2026-11-11, W2 of its
workflow file. Its conditions:

- the repair commit of the review before the merge changed about 40 heads and entries;
- two changes met the re-review rule: a head narrowed, and three `rejected` entries moved;
- the decision-record axis, sent on the whole commit for those two, reported eight findings and two
  lesser points, and judged the two changes that met the rule clean.

The findings by class, as the file lists them, every one in a repair the rule exempts:

- two attributions to the owner, one that dropped a hedge and one that kept a pick whose premise
  had gone; these two are also in the transcript reviewer's remit, which reads every repair made
  before it;
- a `rejected` reason widened from two goals to three;
- a statement answering another question than its head's;
- a rival said to lose both to a fork and to an argument;
- two count commands that did not give their figure.

The classes the file lists count seven findings; which class holds the eighth is not established.
All eight were repaired and folded.

It does not reproduce a loss: the axis read the exempt repairs anyway. What would make it so: a
branch whose repairs met no trigger of the rule and whose record defect was found after the merge,
or by a later round of the axis. The reporter proposed no change on one instance.

The shape a discussion would weigh: a trigger read from the diff rather than judged, such as any
repair commit that changes a design home or a `rejected` home goes to the decision-record axis
alone. The head's argument against a judged trigger does not cover it, and its argument against
"every axis again" covers it only in part. It pulls against the request for fewer review rounds:
a wider trigger adds rounds.

### Why it matters

It strains `design@agent-skills@only-a-head-changing-repair-is-reviewed-again`: if exempt repairs
carry record defects that reach the main branch, the record loses them, against
`goal@design-is-recorded-with-its-arguments`. The cost of leaving a repair's defects to a later
pass is measured in `rejected@agent-skills@repair-defects-left-to-next-pass`: 16 of 38 findings of
an audit sat on text earlier repairs had written.

### What would close it

A second instance, a branch whose exempt repairs carried a record defect found after the merge or
by a later round, which reopens the head's trigger under `skill@knowledge-architect-design` with
the shape above; or the head's trigger rewritten on another argument, which removes this entry's
subject.
