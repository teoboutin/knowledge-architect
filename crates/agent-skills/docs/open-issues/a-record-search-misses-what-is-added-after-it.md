# A record search misses what bears on a part of the work added after it ran

## Summary

The tripwire that guarded `design@agent-skills@one-record-searcher` fired at its second instance:
twice, in separate discussions whose record search ran and whose reports passed
`klarch worklist --verify`, a later review found an entry the work bore on that the search had
graded `unrelated`. Its response reopens that decision, asking what produced the misses.
`rejected@agent-skills@conformance-reader-at-the-checkpoint` is the design its record names as the
candidate if this fired.

## Details

### What

The instances:

- The search of the discussion that designed the development skill and the component skill graded
  `design@knowledge-architect@changelog-reviewed-at-the-release` `unrelated`, and the milestone's
  design-conformance review found that the widened code-claims reviewer the milestone designs rests
  on it, in the commit "[review] Repairs of the plan documents' reviews: slice 1's sites of a fourth
  crate, the retrospective's two clauses, D1 and D2 awaiting the owner".
- The search of the in-change discussion that gave the record searcher `worklist --check` and had
  reviewers write their own reports graded `issue@agent-skills@heads-argued-from-another-harness`
  `unrelated`, its re-entry then naming the next design discussion on the audit's saved workflows.
  The work it was given held no change to those workflows; the change to their report steps was
  added after the search, from the searchers' own findings, with no search of its own. The
  design-conformance and standing-state reviews before the branch's merge found the re-entry met,
  in the commit "[review] Repairs of the review before the merge: `--ci` moves into the gates
  library, three bundled heads split, and the stale statements of the branch repaired".

The suspected mechanism, in one sentence: a search judges the draft it is given, so a thread or a
fix added to the work after it ran is judged by no search unless the session runs one, which
`design@agent-skills@record-searched-before-the-work` asks and nothing checks. Not established for
the first instance, whose work the search was given.

### Why it matters

An entry a search grades `unrelated` is read by the session only by its line, so a miss reaches the
work unweighed, and is found only by a review after the work exists, where it costs rework instead
of a ruling, against `goal@agents-work-without-drift`.

### What would close it

The design discussion that reopens `design@agent-skills@one-record-searcher` on the causes the
tripwire's response named: the size of an agent's load, a split of a search unit, the reading, the
quotation a verdict rests on, or a thread or a fix on a subject the search's work did not cover;
the decision kept, changed or reversed there, with
`rejected@agent-skills@conformance-reader-at-the-checkpoint` weighed again.

### Re-entry

That discussion, which the owner schedules; or the next design discussion that changes the record
search or the grounding's steps, whichever comes first.
