# A member recorded directly, as covered by its head's argument, lay beyond that argument

## Summary

`design@agent-skills@extension-follows-the-ground` records a new member of a head directly where
the head's argument covers it, on the premise that a session judges that coverage as the owner
would. A review in a project that installs the workflow named two members recorded that way that
the argument did not cover. The tripwire that guarded the premise fired on it, on the owner's
ruling, and this entry carries it.

## Details

### What

- The tripwire, deleted from `register@agent-skills@tripwire` when it fired: "Fires when: the
  owner, or a review, names one member recorded directly, as covered by its head's argument, that
  the argument does not cover." Its response: reopen the decision, "with the rival that routes by
  the scope of the owner's approval among the candidates", `rejected@agent-skills@member-routed-by-approval-scope`.
- The instance that fired it: a pin move of thaum to 0.8.0, whose migration asks that each
  `rejected` entry be cited from the heads whose argument covers it. The session added a sentence to
  each of two heads so that they would cite entries their argument did not cover, without the design
  skill; the decision-record reviewer found both to be bundles the backstop of `primer@design-heads`
  should have sent there. Repaired before the merge.
- Two more instances of the same act, in the files of the intake of 2026-10-24: two rules added to
  a head under a relocation said to add no decision, in this repository's branch that built
  `klarch init`; a brief that added to a project's subagents a member a head lists by case, without
  reading the head, in thaum. Each caught by a review before the merge.

The suspected mechanism, in one sentence: a session that needs a citation or a rule to read well
judges its own addition covered, and nothing before the review reads that judgement.

Re-entry point: the design discussion this entry asks for.

### Why it matters

A member beyond the argument enters a head with no deliberation, against
`goal@knowledge-architect@design-is-recorded-with-its-arguments`; here a reviewer caught each one,
at a review round each.

### What would close it

A discussion under `skill@knowledge-architect-design` that keeps
`design@agent-skills@extension-follows-the-ground`, with the reason the review's catch suffices, or
changes it, with the rival above among the candidates.
