# What makes a proper goal has no design beyond the goal-setting skill's shape of an entry

## Summary

The workflow now sends the owner's intent to goals: an intent that states an outcome is drafted as
a goal, and the expectation set asks the owner to state the project's long-term intent as goals,
investing early. What makes a goal proper, beyond the shape
`skill@knowledge-architect-goal-setting@goal-entry-shape` gives an entry, is left loose on purpose;
the owner plans a design session on it.

## Details

### What

The design discussion that removed the overrule and the fourth entry test, deleted with its plan
document at this work's landing, deferred the question to the owner's next design session. The
owner noted that the goal-setting skill already holds a few leads. The working instrument
meanwhile is the residual fork: a choice recorded as "the owner chose X over Y, which no goal ranks"
is a candidate goal, especially when the same weighing recurs, per
`design@agent-skills@fork-choice-in-weak-form`.

### Why it matters

More of the owner's intent now lands in goals, per `design@agent-skills@heads-stand-on-argument`,
and `rejected@agent-skills@owner-intent-entry-test` keeps the doubt that goals homes swell with local
preferences until goals stop reading as what the project is for, against
`goal@knowledge-architect@agents-get-a-complete-workflow`.

### What would close it

A design discussion under `skill@knowledge-architect-design`, on the owner's word, on what makes a
goal proper, its outcome recorded in the goal-setting skill and its design home, or the owner's
word that the skill's shape of an entry is enough.
