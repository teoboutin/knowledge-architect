# The retrospective intake puts to the owner a finding whose outcome the argument settles

## Summary

The owner's goal `goal@knowledge-architect@the-owner-decides`, as the goal-standard work rewords it,
gives the owner a closed list of what is the owner's, "and nothing else". The installed
`skill@knowledge-architect-retrospective-intake` offers the owner an outcome for every finding, one
whose fix the argument from the goals and the record settles included, such as a typo.

## Details

### What

Found by the goal reviewer's draft-mode review of the goals' first batch on the branch of
`spec@plans@goal-standard-and-review`. The owner ruled that the closed list stands and that the
intake comes into line, in a change of its own.

### Why it matters

A question the argument settles, put to the owner, spends the owner's ruling on what is not the
owner's, against `goal@knowledge-architect@the-owner-decides` and
`goal@knowledge-architect@agents-work-without-drift`.

### What would close it

the argument settles, reported to the owner, and put to the owner only what the closed list holds;
written under `skill@knowledge-architect-agent-configuration` and reviewed.
