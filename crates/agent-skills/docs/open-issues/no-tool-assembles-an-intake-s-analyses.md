# No tool assembles an intake's analyses from its drafts, which the owner takes in

## Summary

A retrospective intake of many files writes one analysis per received file, each cluster in the
first file by stem that holds a member and a pointer section in every other, then the lines it owes:
one line per section for the owner, the list of decided outcomes for the commit message, and the
count of `Outcome` lines. Each follows from the drafts and the tree with no judgement, and two
intakes in a row carried them by a script of their own. The owner took the task in at the intake of
twenty files.

## Details

### What

- The rules the scripts applied: `skill@knowledge-architect-retrospective-intake@ground-before-judging`
  point 3 (the host by stem, the pointer sections) and
  `skill@knowledge-architect-retrospective-intake@after-the-ruling` (the message's list of decided
  outcomes).
- The reports: the standing answers on scripted tasks and on tools of the workflow file of the
  intake of forty-seven files, 2026-11-15, a Python script of about 100 lines; the intake of twenty
  files, 2026-11-25, built its per-section list and its decided list by a script again. Analysed in
  the section `intake-assembly-by-script` of the analysis of the intake of forty-seven files'
  workflow file, in the commit that adds the analyses of the intake of twenty files under
  `path@knowledge-architect@docs/retrospective-reports/`, where the owner ruled it taken in.
- A trap such a tool meets: an `Outcome` line copied into the commit message's list of decided
  outcomes can point "above" to the section's item h, which the message does not hold; the intake of
  twenty files carried two such lines.
- Bearing on it: `issue@agent-skills@an-intake-s-lines-per-section-go-in-the-conversation-at-any-count`,
  the lines such a tool would write; `goal@tasks-a-tool-does-better-are-the-tool-s`.

### Why it matters

Every intake of more than a handful of files repeats the assembly, and a hand script can misplace a
cluster or drop a pointer section; the task is a tool's by `goal@tasks-a-tool-does-better-are-the-tool-s`,
taken in by the owner's choice, per `goal@generic-tools-and-procedures-are-shipped`.

### What would close it

A design argued under `skill@knowledge-architect-design` that chooses the tool's shape, a command of
`klarch` or a step of a saved workflow of the intake, then that tool shipped, and the intake skill
pointing to it.
