# An intake's lines per section go in the conversation at any count

## Summary

The intake skill gives the owner one line per section of its analyses in the conversation. At the
intake of forty files that made 438 lines, and the session wrote them to a file and gave its path
with a summary by outcome kind. Whether the conversation still serves the instruction's reason at
hundreds of lines is not measured, and every rival changes the words of a head.

## Details

### What

The instruction, `skill@knowledge-architect-retrospective-intake@analysis-and-ruling`: "Then give
the owner the full path of each file, and in the conversation one line per section: its ids, its
kind of item d, the proposal in a few words, and who holds the outcome". Its reason is
`design@agent-skills@intake-outcome-authority`: "the summary in the conversation says of each line
who holds it, so that the owner sees every finding and its kind and can bring a fact, an argument
or an intent to any of them before the merge". The source holds no `%%` comment at that sentence.

Instances:

- the intake of forty files, 2026-11-07, W2 of its workflow file: 237 sections, 132 with an
  outcome and 105 pointers, 438 lines; the session wrote the lines to a file, gave its path, and
  gave a summary by outcome kind in the conversation. The file proposes the lines in the
  conversation, or in a file the session names where they exceed what a message holds, with a
  count per outcome kind in the message;
- the intake of forty-seven files, 2026-11-14, meets the same case: 307 sections, 172 with an
  outcome.

The suspected mechanism, in one sentence: a fixed place for the lines, chosen for a batch of a few
files, does not scale with the count of sections.

The rivals:

- the lines in a file the session names past a threshold, with a count per outcome kind in the
  conversation;
- the lines always in a file whose path the conversation gives, with the counts;
- no change: one long message.

A summary in place of the lines, with no file, is ruled out: the owner loses the path to each
section, which the head's reason needs. Not measured: whether a message of 438 lines reaches the
owner whole and readable in the harness.

Re-entry point: the branch of `issue@agent-skills@the-intake-analysis-s-sections-on-one-branch`,
which changes the same section of the skill and the intake heads; its design discussion under
`skill@knowledge-architect-design`, since a file in place of the conversation contradicts the words
of `design@agent-skills@intake-outcome-authority`, per the backstop of `primer@design-heads`.

### Why it matters

The head's reason is that the owner sees every finding before the merge, per
`goal@the-owner-decides`. A message the owner cannot read whole defeats it as surely as a summary
does; a session that moves the lines to a file meanwhile acts against the skill's words. Until the
entry closes, an intake gives the lines in the conversation, as the sentence says.

### What would close it

The measurement of whether a message of several hundred lines reaches the owner whole and readable
in the harness, then a decision among the rivals, recorded in
`design@agent-skills@intake-outcome-authority` and the skill's sentence, or a ruling that the
sentence stands.
