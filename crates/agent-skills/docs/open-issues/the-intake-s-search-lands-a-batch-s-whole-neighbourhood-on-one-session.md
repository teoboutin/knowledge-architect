# A retrospective intake handles many unrelated findings at once, so its grounding may still load one session beyond one reading

## Summary

A retrospective intake receives a batch of files, each holding findings on unrelated subjects, and
grounds them in one search of the whole record. The owner doubts that one verdict file per cluster
of findings, which `design@query@occasion-shaped-output` states, is enough, and believes the intake
may need a different procedure with more steps to partition its work. The discussion
waits for the first intake run under a text that lets agents establish any item.

## Details

### What

The instances, measured before the verdict shape, as the analyses of the commit "[intake] The
analyses of forty received retrospective files, three outcomes for the owner" record them: the
intake of seventeen files graded 200 entries `bears` and 164 `may-bear`; the intake of forty files 333
and 249, which nine checking subagents read in parts, as
`rejected@agent-skills@relevance-grades-read-by-the-session` also states; and an intake of thirteen
files, whose session read 64 standing entries whole, about 167 KB, as
`design@agent-skills@one-record-searcher` stated in the tree of the commit "[intake] The analyses of
forty received retrospective files, three outcomes for the owner".

The owner's statement, in the design discussion of the record search: the search suits the design
skill better than the design audit and the intake; the intake may have a deeper problem, since its
nature has it handle many unrelated small problems, so it often ends up covering the whole design
record; one verdict file per subject of the work may not fully solve it, and a significantly
different procedure, with more steps to partition the work, might be needed; the scope of that
discussion stayed limited, to see how the situation improves at the next intake.

The intake of forty-seven files, 2026-11-14, the first run on one verdict file per cluster, as the
commit "[intake] The analyses of forty-seven received retrospective files, five outcomes for the
owner" records it: 184 findings in 120 subjects, and 18 searchers. Several searchers read the files
through an inventory and the findings bearing on their entries rather than whole, so an `unrelated`
verdict rests on that reading. Nine checking agents established items a to h by group of subjects,
against the text of the time, which kept d to h to the session; ten verified and repaired their
drafts; the session decided each outcome. Its workflow file,
2026-11-15-knowledge-architect-intake-forty-seven-files-klarch-workflow, adds that the received files
held 46,922 words, above the search's default bound of 40,000, which the session raised to 90,000,
and that the session's context bore every agent's report.

The partition that the intake's text gives: agents may establish any of the items a to h of
`skill@knowledge-architect-retrospective-intake@what-to-establish`, briefed as
`skill@knowledge-architect-retrospective-intake@intake-subagents` says, on the judgement of
`design@agent-skills@work-divided-by-judgement`, and the session decides each outcome, per
`design@agent-skills@intake-outcome-authority`. Not observed: an intake run under that text, its
brief included. The search itself is not partitioned: every received file is the work of every
searcher.

The suspected mechanism, in one sentence: an intake's work is the union of its findings'
neighbourhoods, so whatever the search's shape, everything the batch touches reaches the one session
that holds the batch, or the one work that every searcher reads.

Re-entry point: the retrospective of the first intake the owner runs after the commit "[agent-skills]
A retrospective intake's items a to h may be established by agents, divided by judgement" reaches the
main branch, or the owner's word.

### Why it matters

An intake whose session cannot carry its grounding judges outcomes on less than the record, against
`goal@agents-work-without-drift`, and spends the main session's context on reading
that `goal@session-orchestrates-the-work` hands to dispatched agents.

### What would close it

A design discussion under `skill@knowledge-architect-design`, at the re-entry point, that keeps the
intake's procedure, its items divided by judgement, with the measured load of that intake, or that
changes how the search, or the reading of its verdicts and of the agents' reports, is partitioned.
