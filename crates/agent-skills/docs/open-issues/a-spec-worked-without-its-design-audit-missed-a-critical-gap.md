# A spec worked without its design audit, as the rule allows, missed a critical gap an audit would have listed

## Summary

The planning skill runs a spec's design audit only when a fresh session starts its work or the main
branch has moved. The work of `spec@plans@graded-entries-and-id-lookup` skipped it, as the rule
allows, and its review before the merge found a critical gap an audit would have listed: a shape
the tree refuted. The tripwire that watched this premise fired and left its home.

## Details

### What

The spec decided that the assembly of the record search gives a goal by its grades alone, "since the
session reads every goals home whole". The tree the spec was written against refuted that for one
of the assembly's readers: the design audit of `skill@knowledge-architect-planning@working-a-slice`,
point 1, reads the Component's `CLAUDE.md`, its design home and its `rejected` home, and no goals
home; and `design@agent-skills@searcher-grades-goals` names only the design grounding and the intake
as readings of every goals home. So, as built, the design audit would act on a goal graded `bears`
whose text it never read. The self-consistency axis of the review before the merge found it; the
spec's own reviews, eight axes, did not. The repair, in the branch's review repairs: the design
audit and the intake read with `show` each goal the assembly gives by its grades alone.

The gap is critical by the tripwire's own test: the work's result, the design audit's reading, is
wrong without its repair.

The tripwire that fired, deleted with this entry's opening, read: "a review finds, in the work of a
spec that skipped its design audit as the rule allows, a critical gap an audit would have listed: a
shape the tree refutes, a name the code does not have, or a standing entry the work bears on that
the grounding did not meet." Its response: open a `defect` naming the spec and the change, and
reopen the condition for the audit, with a design audit for the work of every spec, as for every
slice, the first candidate.

Not established: whether a design audit would have found it. The spec's own design-conformance
review read the spec against the record and the tree and did not; an audit reads the spec against
the code and the design homes, where the installed planning skill is code of this repository.

### Why it matters

It strains the instruction of `skill@knowledge-architect-planning@working-a-slice` that skips a
spec's design audit when the converging session works it on an unmoved main branch, argued in the
`%%` line beside it, against `goal@knowledge-architect@agents-work-without-drift`, which asks that
a departure be caught before it merges: here a review caught it, after the work was built.

### What would close it

A design discussion under `skill@knowledge-architect-design` that reopens the condition: a design
audit for the work of every spec, as for every slice, the first candidate; or keeps it, with this
instance weighed, and records the reason. Raised on the owner's word.
