# A goal the draft-mode review read was reworded again for a defect the goal standard names, twice

## Summary

The tripwire that watched a goal the reviewer read passing with a defect the goal standard names
fired at its second instance and left its home, so
`design@agent-skills@goal-reviewer-agent` and `design@agent-skills@goal-draft-shows-reach` are
reopened. A goal read by the goal reviewer in draft mode before the owner ruled still reached the
tree with a clause the owner had not ruled in knowingly.

## Details

### What

- The first instance, recorded in that tripwire: `goal@knowledge-architect@the-owner-decides`,
  reworded at the third layout round of the goal-standard branch for a rule-2 defect.
- The second: `goal@agent-skills@only-needed-instructions`. The draft-mode review recorded in
  `git show 0d2668e8^:docs/goal-reviews/2026-10-31-review-before-merge.md` flagged its reason
  requirement only as unmeasured, rule 6, minor; the goal was written with "with the reason for each
  instruction", and the branch of the spec of the retrospective intake deciding by authority removed the clause on the
  owner's word that it was wrong and missed when the owner read the drafts: a reach the owner had not
  ruled out knowingly, which rule 3 of the goal standard names.
- The re-review loop, met in the same session: three draft-mode reviews of one batch each found new
  defects, until the owner stopped the loop. `skill@knowledge-architect-goal-setting` reviews again
  a goal the owner rewords, with the whole batch, and states no stop;
  `design@agent-skills@whole-set-rounds` stops the review of the whole goal set only.

The suspected mechanism, in one sentence: the reviewer reports a clause under the rule its wording
breaks most visibly, and a reach shown as a minor measure finding does not reach the owner as a
question of intent.

Re-entry point: a design discussion under `skill@knowledge-architect-design`, on the owner's word
or when work next changes the goal reviewer's definition.

### Why it matters

A goal binds outright, so a clause the owner did not intend rules work out with the owner's
authority, against `goal@knowledge-architect@the-owner-decides`.

### What would close it

A discussion that keeps `design@agent-skills@goal-reviewer-agent` and
`design@agent-skills@goal-draft-shows-reach` with the reason, or changes how a draft shows each
clause's reach to the owner; and that decides whether goal-setting's review of a reworded goal
ends, and on what.
