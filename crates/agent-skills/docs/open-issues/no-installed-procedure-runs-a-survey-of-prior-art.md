# No installed procedure says how a survey of prior art runs, which the owner takes in

## Summary

The design skill says when to propose a survey of prior art, not how to run one. A session of a
project that installs the workflow ran one: two open engines read from shallow clones, their code
quoted with line numbers. The owner took in the generic part of that procedure at the intake of
forty-seven files, and reports having plans for it.

## Details

### What

- The design skill, `skill@knowledge-architect-design`, proposes a survey and gives no procedure
  for one; a finding read in another project goes to a grounding file of its Component, per the root
  CLAUDE.md's knowledge table.
- The report: the standing answers of the workflow file of that project's session of 2026-11-13 on
  a long draw, generic, by its words, "to any project that implements a specification other engines
  implement". Analysed in the section on the tools and procedures the batch reports, in the analysis
  of 2026-11-12-knowledge-architect-finding-repairs-klarch-workflow, which "[intake] The forty-seven
  retrospective analyses leave, every outcome carried out" removed, where the owner ruled it taken
  in.

### Why it matters

A survey read by each session in its own way leaves each step to the session, which
`goal@tasks-a-tool-does-better-are-the-tool-s` and
`goal@generic-tools-and-procedures-are-shipped` take from it once the owner takes the procedure in.

### What would close it

A procedure designed under `skill@knowledge-architect-design`, on the owner's plans, and shipped
in the installed text.
