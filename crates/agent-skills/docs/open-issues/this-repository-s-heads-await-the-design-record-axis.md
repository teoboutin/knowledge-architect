# This repository's design heads that cite the owner's words await a pass of the design-record axis under the rule on what a head stands on

## Summary

The rule on what a design head stands on now reads an approval as no ground, a pick at a fork as a
choice in its weak form, and an intent as a goal to draft only where it states a purpose, a scope or
a ranking; and a head that cites a goal states, in one sentence beside the reference, the constraint
it derives from that goal. This repository pins the checker it builds, so no pin move opens the
audit that re-grounds its heads: several still name the owner's intent, or the removed fourth entry
test, as their ground, and many cite a goal with no constraint stated beside the reference.

## Details

### What

The changelog's Migration entry sends a consuming project to
`skill@knowledge-architect-project-audit@design-record-axis`. This repository's heads that cite the
owner's intent as their ground include `design@agent-skills@no-untested-snippet-is-authority`,
whose ranking was confirmed under the removed entry test, and a head of
`path@model@docs/design/anchors.md` whose ground reads "the owner's intent"; a search for "the owner's intent" and "on the
owner's word" over the design homes lists the rest. The heads that cite a goal are listed by
`cargo klarch show` on each goal of `register@goal`; a head that a change of
this repository touched since the rule was written states the constraint already, and the others
do not.

### Why it matters

A head whose ground names an intent no goal states reads, under the new rule, as a goal to draft
where the intent is of the three kinds, and otherwise as an argument; a later session cannot tell
whether to argue against it or ask the owner. A head that cites a goal with no constraint beside it
leaves a reader to work out what the goal binds there, which two heads can read two ways. Both are
against
`goal@design-is-recorded-with-its-arguments`.

### What would close it

A pass of the design-record axis over this repository, its outcomes applied: each head re-grounded
on its argument, a fork's choice in its weak form, each intent of the three kinds put on the owner
list as a goal to draft, and each standing head that cites a goal stating its constraint beside the
reference.
