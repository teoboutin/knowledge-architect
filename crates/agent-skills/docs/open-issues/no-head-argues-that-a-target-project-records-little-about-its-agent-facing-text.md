# No design head argues the expectation that a target project records little of its own about its agent-facing text

## Summary

One statement about target projects is argued by no head and rests on no measurement: that a
project which installs the workflow holds few goals or decisions of its own about its agent-facing
text. `design@agent-skills@statement-names-an-act` uses it as a premise.

## Details

### What

The statement: "It is not expected to hold many goals or decisions of its own about its
agent-facing text: knowledge-architect carries most of what concerns that text, in the instructions
the installed text gives, and the project adds what its own activities need." It bound no
instruction by itself. One decision takes it as a premise:
`design@agent-skills@statement-names-an-act` sends to the owner an instruction no agent performs
whose act the project's record does not settle as still wanted or not, on the ground that a project
which installs the workflow seldom records that about its agent-facing text.

Instances seen: none counted. No count of the goals, the decisions and the reasons that thaum or
rootpoint records about its own agent-facing text was taken.

The suspected mechanism, in one sentence: the expectation was written from this repository's own
position, which holds the record of the installed text and so most decisions about agent-facing
text, and was not checked in a project that only installs that text.

Re-entry point: the next design discussion that changes
`design@agent-skills@statement-names-an-act`, or a pass of the agentic-workflow axis of
`skill@knowledge-architect-project-audit` in a target project other than this repository, which
reads that project's own agent-facing text and the reasons recorded beside it.

### Why it matters

Where the expectation is false in a target project, its record often settles whether the act of an
instruction no agent performs is still wanted, and the routing of
`design@agent-skills@statement-names-an-act` then needs no such premise; where it is true, the
premise is a fact the head can state with its measurement. Until then the head leans on a
statement nobody checked.

### What would close it

A count, in thaum and in rootpoint, of the reasons each records beside its own agent-facing text
and of the heads of its design homes on that text, with the command that takes it. Then the
statement enters `design@agent-skills@statement-names-an-act` as a measured premise, or the head is
argued without it, under `skill@knowledge-architect-design`.
