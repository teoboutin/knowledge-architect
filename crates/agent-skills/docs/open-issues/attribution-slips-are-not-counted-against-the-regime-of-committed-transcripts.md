# The attribution slips of the next release's retrospectives are not counted against the regime of committed transcripts

## Summary

Plan documents now attribute the owner's words in their items and keep the verbatim words in a
committed transcript, as every other durable record attributes them. The hypothesis that the earlier
mix of regimes confused sessions was weighed on a baseline of slips; nothing has yet counted the
slips under the new regime, to compare with that baseline.

## Details

### What

The baseline, from the intake of forty received retrospective files: thirteen of their 23 workflow
files report slips against the attribution rules of `primer@design-heads`,
`primer@owner-word-premise` or `primer@question-authority`, at least 24 slips, each caught before
the merge. By kind: an approval or an answer recorded as a ruling, 10; an act or an argument of the
session attributed to the owner, 6; a hedge widened, 1; a quotation, 5, in three files; a premise
not shown, 2. Only the quotation kind bears on the owner's hypothesis, which the owner gave hedged,
that the planning skill's demand for exact quotations probably confused sessions. The major slips,
a decision attributed to the owner that the owner did not make, were in six files, and one slip
changed what was built.

The count owed: the same kinds, over the retrospective files received for the first release tagged
after the merge of the slice that made plan documents directories, counted per workflow file as the
baseline is, and compared with the baseline.

### Why it matters

`design@agent-skills@attribution-replaces-quotation` now holds for a plan document's items, on the
owner's report and on the five quotation slips, and
`rejected@agent-skills@owner-words-verbatim-in-plan-items` is kept on the doubt that an attribution
loses a scope or a hedge a quotation showed. Without the count, neither the decision nor the doubt
meets the evidence that would confirm or reopen it, and a rise in misattributions, which records a
firmer or a different ruling than the owner gave, goes unseen, against
`goal@design-is-recorded-with-its-arguments`.

### Trigger

The retrospective files received for the first release tagged after the merge of the slice that
made plan documents directories have all been analysed under docs/retrospective-reports/. Then
count the slips by kind, compare them with the baseline, record the comparison in this entry's
closing commit, and reopen `design@agent-skills@attribution-replaces-quotation` if the
misattribution kinds rose.
