# The attribution slips of the next release's retrospectives are not counted against the regime of committed transcripts

## Summary

Plan documents now attribute the owner's words in their items and keep the verbatim words of their
design discussions, up to the last convergence checkpoint, in committed transcripts, as every other durable record attributes them. The hypothesis that the earlier
mix of regimes confused sessions was weighed on a baseline of slips; nothing has yet counted the
slips under the new regime, to compare with that baseline.

## Details

### What

The baseline, from the intake of forty received retrospective files: thirteen of their 23 workflow
files report slips against the attribution rules of `primer@design-heads`,
`primer@owner-word-premise` or `primer@question-authority`, at least 24 slips, each caught before
the merge. By kind: an approval or an answer recorded as a ruling, 10; an act or an argument of the
session attributed to the owner, 6; a hedge widened, 1; a quotation, 5, in three files; a premise
not shown, 2. Only the quotation kind bears on the owner's hypothesis, which the owner gave hedged,
that the planning skill's demand for exact quotations probably confused sessions. The major slips,
a decision attributed to the owner that the owner did not make, were in six files, and one slip
changed what was built.

The count owed: the same kinds, over the first batch of retrospective files an intake receives
whose sessions ran a release tagged after the merge of the slice that made plan documents
directories, counted per workflow file as the baseline is, and compared with the baseline. The
slips on rulings given after a design discussion's last convergence checkpoint, at a plan review
or at the review before the merge, are counted apart: such a ruling is in no committed transcript,
per `design@agent-skills@committed-transcript`, so its attribution has no verbatim copy to be
checked against.

Instances before the trigger, from the retrospective files received at the intake of twenty files,
each caught before its merge. Under the new regime, four workflow files of this repository's
sessions built from source after 518cf47c: an approval recorded as a ruling, 1 (a default
recorded as ruled, 2026-11-16-knowledge-architect-record-search-workflow-klarch-workflow); an act or
a reading of the session attributed to the owner, 1
(2026-11-22-knowledge-architect-owner-rulings-and-title-rules-klarch-workflow); a hedge widened, in
2 files: the owner's hedged words stated without their hedges in the owner-rulings file, and
"would have", "a lot more" and the past scope of an owner's report dropped in three agents' drafts
of one branch (2026-11-16-knowledge-architect-doc-gate-and-lints-klarch-workflow); a quotation of
the owner in a commit message, 2 (the record-search file and
2026-11-25-knowledge-architect-windows-and-macos-gates-klarch-workflow). Under the old regime, one
file of thaum on 0.10.0: "the owner chose" written for approvals of the session's default four
times in commit messages and at six places of a milestone document
(2026-11-15-thaum-cast-permissions-klarch-workflow).

### Why it matters

`design@agent-skills@attribution-replaces-quotation` now holds for a plan document's items, on the
owner's report and on the five quotation slips, and
`rejected@agent-skills@owner-words-verbatim-in-plan-items` is kept on the doubt that an attribution
loses a scope or a hedge a quotation showed. Without the count, neither the decision nor the doubt
meets the evidence that would confirm or reopen it, and a rise in misattributions, which records a
firmer or a different ruling than the owner gave, goes unseen, against
`goal@design-is-recorded-with-its-arguments`.

### Trigger

The intake of the first batch of retrospective files whose sessions ran a release tagged after the
merge of the slice that made plan documents directories, its analyses written under
`path@knowledge-architect@docs/retrospective-reports/`. That intake counts the slips of its batch by
kind, those on rulings given after the last checkpoint apart, compares them with the baseline, records the comparison in this entry's
closing commit, and reopens `design@agent-skills@attribution-replaces-quotation` if the
misattribution kinds rose.
