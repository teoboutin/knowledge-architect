# Goal-setting has no step to discover an intent the owner does not hold yet

## Summary

`skill@knowledge-architect-goal-setting@drawing-out-intent` starts by asking the owner to state
their intent. The owner reported that, for a new Component, the intent did not exist at the start
in a form the owner could state, and formed through the draft rounds, each time a consequence was
shown. No step shows the owner consequences before the draft. The owner proposed a step before
the draft, its shape open.

## Details

### What

The text, in `path@agent-skills@content/skills/goal-setting/SKILL.md`: step 1, "Ask the owner to
state their intent for the Component, and any goals they already have, in their own words. Three
questions help"; step 2, "Propose further goals from the existing documentation, or from the code".
`design@agent-skills@goal-draft-shows-reach` shows consequences, but only in the boundary block
after a draft.

The instances:

- the slice that put `cli` on top, 2026-11-06. The slice's spec said the new Component's goals were
  drafted and put to the owner; the session read it as the draft's source, skipped step 1, and put
  labelled questions on candidate goals. The goal written was a secondary one under a head claiming
  the whole purpose; the owner's main purpose surfaced after the review's transcript axis, and cost
  four more draft rounds and a merge-mode round. The owner then reported that the intent formed when
  a consequence was shown, by four items: a goal shown alone, with a head claiming the Component's
  whole purpose; a criterion tested as a table of every crate, which showed the draft had the
  default backwards; a list of what a candidate rule would also rule out, which the owner corrected;
  and the owner's own open question on which crates enter the program, stated while answering. The
  owner proposed a goal discussion step before the draft and the reviewer's rounds, its shape
  open; the session proposed that it be argued under `skill@knowledge-architect-design`;
- the slice that moved the consumers out, 2026-11-06. A milestone sentence had the session draft
  the goals; two of three Components had no statement of the owner, the session drafted from the
  design heads, and 42 of the 61 findings of three review rounds were `intent`, many on goals
  shaped as the code stands, which `design@agent-skills@goal-drafted-to-move-the-tree` refuses.

Two parts: a lapse, twice, on step 1, each session reading a plan's "drafted by the session" as
leave to skip it, which the text does not give and which takes no text, per the lapse rule; and a
missing capability, on the owner's report: an intent the owner has not formed cannot be stated on
request.

The rivals, none chosen: keep step 1 and let the reviewer's rounds reach the same discussion, as
in the first instance, which adds no step and costs rounds run after the design the goals should
have shaped; step 1 widened to show consequences; a step before step 2. The cost to weigh: a
discussion per Component whose goals are drafted.

The neighbour, `issue@agent-skills@the-goal-standard-does-not-say-what-a-goal-is-worth-or-what-it-is-for`,
is a design question on what a goal is for; it does not ask how an intent is drawn out.

The suspected mechanism, in one sentence: an intent forms when the owner sees what a goal would
rule in or out, and the procedure asks for it before showing anything.

Re-entry point: a design discussion under `skill@knowledge-architect-design`, on the owner's word or
when work next changes how goal-setting draws out intent, held with or beside the neighbour's.

Source: W1 of the analysis 2026-11-06-knowledge-architect-cli-on-top-klarch-workflow, with W7 of
2026-11-06-knowledge-architect-consumers-out-klarch-workflow and P3 of
2026-11-06-knowledge-architect-consumers-out, in the commit "[intake] The analyses of forty
received retrospective files, three outcomes for the owner".

### Why it matters

A goal drafted before the owner's intent has formed records a secondary purpose or the code as it
stands, and the correction costs draft rounds after the design it should have shaped, against
`goal@agent-skills@goals-state-the-owner-s-intent` and `goal@knowledge-architect@the-owner-decides`.

### What would close it

A discussion under `skill@knowledge-architect-design` that chooses a shape among the rivals above,
or keeps step 1 as it is with the reason, and records it in the goal-setting skill and in the head
that argues it.
