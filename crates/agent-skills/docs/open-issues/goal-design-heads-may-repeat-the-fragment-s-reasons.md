# Several goal-design heads may argue only a rule the goal standard's fragment states, and repeat its reasons

## Summary

The "Goals" section of `register@agent-skills@design` holds a head for each rule of the goal
standard. The standard is stated once, in `path@agent-skills@fragments/goal-standard.md`, whose `%%`
comments carry each rule's cases and reasons; the build copies it whole into its two hosts, which is
no restatement. A head that argues only a rule the fragment alone states may fail test 2 of
`primer@design-heads`, a decision stated by one text having one site whose reason lives beside it,
and repeat reasons the fragment's comments already carry, which drift apart.

## Details

### What

Which heads of `path@agent-skills@docs/design/goals.md` argue only a rule of the fragment, and
whether each earns its head. Heads whose decision more than one text states, such as the grades, the
stop and the reviewer's modes, which the skill and the agent both state, plausibly earn theirs. The
session raised it while the owner asked why the section holds so many heads; the owner kept the
heads as they are in the goal-standard branch, with no relocation that day, and did not judge the
possible defect.

### Why it matters

A head that repeats a `%%` reason of the fragment is a second copy, which drifts from the first, the
cost the owner named for the goals homes' heads that restated goal-setting. It strains
`design@agent-skills@goal-standard-rules`.

### What would close it

A pass over the section, head by head, under `skill@knowledge-architect-decision-recording`: each
head that argues only a fragment rule either kept on a reason it gives, or folded into the
fragment's comment and `design@agent-skills@goal-standard-rules`, the relocation reviewed by the
fidelity axis.
