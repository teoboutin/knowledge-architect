# Eight tripwires of this repository respond with a direct action, which the tripwire shape does not name

## Summary

`skill@knowledge-architect-issue-tracking@tripwire-entry` gives a tripwire's response as "reopen
the decision by its reference, or open an issue of a named kind". Eight responses in this
repository's tripwires homes name a direct action instead, or before one of the two.

## Details

### What

Found by a search at the retrospective intake of 2026-10-24: in every tripwires home, the
`**Response:**` lines whose text does not open with "open" or "reopen".

- In `register@agent-skills@tripwire`: `tripwire@agent-skills@owner-intent-stripped`, "restore the
  owner's ground, attributed, and reopen …"; `tripwire@agent-skills@rule-title-wider-than-its-argument`,
  "repair the title, and reopen …"; `tripwire@agent-skills@rarer-rule-in-the-model`, "remove the
  rule from the section, and reopen with the owner …".
- In `register@core@tripwire`: `tripwire@core@candidate-rule-silence`, "widen the candidate rule to
  the shape found"; `tripwire@core@checker-source-literals-are-data-self-location`,
  `tripwire@core@checker-source-literals-are-data-whole-source-scope` and
  `tripwire@core@checker-source-literals-are-data-purpose`, each opening with an account of what the
  firing means; `tripwire@core@inputs-builder-needed`, "add a builder on `Gathered` … and open a
  `todo`".
- Not established: whether each response's action is the work an issue would carry, or a repair the
  firing session makes at once.

The same question was met in a thaum session, where the owner ruled a direct action as a
tripwire's response and its rewording to the shape went back to the owner; see
`design@agent-skills@tripwire-wording-is-the-agents`, under which a response's wording is the
agent's.

### Why it matters

A response that acts at once leaves nothing open between the firing and the work, which an issue
entry holds, and the shape the checker's readers expect is not the one these entries use; the
standing-state reviewer reads each against the shape.

### What would close it

Each of the eight brought to the shape, as a rewording the agent makes and lists under "Decided",
per `design@agent-skills@tripwire-wording-is-the-agents`; or the shape widened to admit a direct
action, argued under `skill@knowledge-architect-design`.
