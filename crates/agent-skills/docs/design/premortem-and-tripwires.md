# Agent skills design: Premortems and tripwires

## Premortems and tripwires

### The session records or drops each premortem cause by the standard at the end of the work, and the owner overrules by reading it `##tripwire-recording-authority`

A premortem names causes and puts none to the owner. At the end of the work that implements the
decision, the harvest on the full path or the commit that records the decision on the in-change
path, the session judges each cause by the standard of
`design@agent-skills@tripwire-admission-test`, on the wording the cause has then, writes those that
pass in the tripwires home of the Component that owns the decision, and lists every cause with its
outcome in its turn's "Decided" section and in the record of the work, the harvest's or the
in-change commit message. A tripwire names the decision it guards, and a head exists from the
landing of its work, per `design@agent-skills@design-home-is-built-intent`, so the tripwire is
written with the head.

Whether a risk is worth watching trades the cost of watching against the risk. The standard ranks
it: a cause it admits and one it excludes are each decided by a named condition, so no fork
remains, and the question is the session's, per `primer@question-authority` and
`design@agent-skills@owner-rules-at-a-fork`. The owner stays informed of each outcome and overrules
any of them by reading the work, per `goal@knowledge-architect@the-owner-decides`, and rules only on
what the goals and the record leave open, per `goal@knowledge-architect@agents-work-without-drift`.
The rival, the owner ruling on each cause, was measured: of 66 premortem causes the owner ruled on
in this repository, the owner dropped 23, and about 45 of the 64 tripwires this repository then
held watched a failure that a review, a test, the checker or the owner caught anyway, by a
subagent's reading of each entry, whose count the spec of the tripwire standard keeps, deleted at
its landing; the readings per entry were not kept.
The rival that keeps each admitted cause with the owner, as a question with a default, halves the
rulings rather than removing them, while the standard ranks what it admits as it ranks what it
excludes. This is no grant of judgement within a boundary, which
`rejected@agent-skills@delegated-decision-state` refuses: it is the rule of
`primer@question-authority` applied once the record ranks the question. The design this decision
did not keep: `rejected@agent-skills@owner-rules-each-tripwire`.

### A tripwire stands on the standard whoever proposed it, and the session removes one a condition excludes `##tripwire-stands-on-the-standard`

A recorded tripwire's premise, firing clause, response, re-entry point and horizon change on the
session's judgement under the standard of `design@agent-skills@tripwire-admission-test`, while it
watches the same cause. The session removes a recorded tripwire that a condition of the standard
excludes, the commit naming the condition, as it drops a cause at recording, per
`design@agent-skills@tripwire-recording-authority`; in a project audit the removal is applied in
the branch and listed in the report, grouped by condition. Each change and each removal is listed
in the turn's "Decided" section, per `design@agent-skills@decisions-reported-to-the-owner`, and the
owner overrules by reading it. A removal on the doubt's weight alone is the owner's, since it rests
on the owner's own weighing of what the owner would see. A cause the owner proposes, or one derived
from the owner's words, is judged by the standard like any other: the owner's word alone admits no
tripwire and keeps none. A ruling of the owner that a tripwire restates, a bound or an exception,
lives in a design head, which the tripwire cites. A tripwire also leaves by the other exits of its
lifecycle, per `design@agent-skills@tripwire-horizon`, and on the owner's word.

A tripwire is no decision: it watches one premise of a decision, and its only value is the fact it
delivers, which the standard measures whoever proposed it. A ruling of the owner bears on a
tripwire in one of three ways, and none lives only in the tripwire. A ruling to watch a cause is
the question the standard ranks: the first pass of the tripwire axis on this repository removed
entries the owner had ruled kept, with the owner's approval, as the commit "[audit] Pass 1 of the
tripwire axis applied: every tripwire kept has a horizon, and 20 leave" lists. A ruling on the decision itself, such
as the explanation of `check --fix`'s refusal that `design@agent-skills@checker-model-in-primer`
keeps in the primer, lives in that head, and a narrowing of the tripwire that dropped it would make
its clause fire on the decision working, which condition 3 excludes. A ruling on what fires one
tripwire, such as the owner's that a rule a review finds and its own branch removes does not fire
`tripwire@agent-skills@rarer-rule-in-the-model`, is a case of the rule on slips, which the
standard states as the owner's. So a narrowing or a removal of
a tripwire changes no ruling of the owner, unlike a head, which can record the owner's intent, per
`design@agent-skills@head-ground-is-the-argument`. The owner rules only on what the goals and the
record leave open, per `goal@knowledge-architect@agents-work-without-drift`, and is informed of each
removal and can overrule it, per `goal@knowledge-architect@the-owner-decides`. The owner named the
wording and the small changes of scope of tripwires among the rulings a clearer standard should
let the session's judgement cover. Of the two shapes that meet these goals, the owner chose that
the session removes a tripwire a condition excludes wherever it is found, over a split that keeps an
audit's removals on the owner's list, a removal on the doubt's weight excepted, and ruled
that a tripwire grounded on the owner's word gets no standing the standard does not give it.
Keeping each removal with the owner brings back part of
`rejected@agent-skills@owner-rules-each-tripwire`.

### A cause becomes a tripwire only where its failure is silent, its evidence lands where tripwires are read, it is what the decision forbids, and its numbers have a basis `##tripwire-admission-test`

The standard is one text, the fragment `path@agent-skills@fragments/tripwire-standard.md`: four
conditions, all owed; a rule that a slip is no firing, a clause on how sessions follow an
instruction firing on a recurrence in separate pieces of work or on one instance that reached the
main branch with no procedure reporting it; and a doubt that keeps the cause with a short horizon,
unless the owner would see the failure anyway.

A tripwire earns its reading only by delivering a fact no other procedure delivers. Condition 1,
silence, is the session's reading of the question the owner asked of past firings: whether the
facts that made a tripwire fire would have been found without it. Read in hindsight over the 12 firings of this repository and the 37 of thaum, 25 were
found and acted on anyway, through a test, a gate, a review doing its own job, a request or the plan
itself, and 8 needed the tripwire, each a failure every procedure counted as success, by a
subagent's hindsight judgement per firing with its commit; the spec this decision landed from keeps
the counts and names the 8, and the judgements per firing were not kept. Condition 2 is the owner's cost of watching: a tripwire whose evidence no reading occasion
holds is read at every merge for nothing; a received retrospective file counts, since the intake
reads every tripwire against it. Condition 3 removes the clause that counts what the decision
instructs, which can fire only on the decision working: the three false alarms of the 49 firings.
Condition 4 removes the number written to make a clause specific, which fires on noise or never:
thaum's `t-event-payload` fired five times, the last three only moving its own bound, inside the
noise of its instrument. That one slip is no firing is the owner's: an agent not following an
instruction once or twice in a session is normal, and reviews exist to catch it. That a recurrence
fires is the session's argument, since a recurrence is how such a premise shows false. The doubt keeps the cause because horizons make the two errors
unequal: a wrong keep costs reading until its horizon, a wrong drop loses the evidence for good.
That a wrong drop costs little where the owner would see the failure anyway is the owner's weight on
a doubt, never a reason to refuse a cause the conditions admit.

The rival, the falsifiability bar alone with the owner filtering, kept every cause precise enough
to fire, whatever it watched: the owner dropped 23 of 66 ruled causes, and about 45 of 64 standing
entries watched a failure caught anyway, per `rejected@agent-skills@owner-rules-each-tripwire`.
The owner ranked the result: that the tripwires admitted are valuable matters more than that every
past useful one stays admitted, so a refinement admitting a dropped useful firing was taken only
where it admitted none of the 25 found anyway, which is the rule on slips' second case.

### Every tripwire names the occasions after which, unfired, it leaves `##tripwire-horizon`

A tripwire carries a fourth line, `**Watches until:**`, naming the first occasions that exercise its
decision, counted by a command over the history from the commit that wrote the line: commits that
change a named path or the guarded head, release tags, audit reports of an axis, plan documents
deleted at their landing, or one event the tree shows. Its length is the writer's judgement;
`standing` is for a guarantee whose failure stays silent and permanent, with its reason. The
standing-state reviewer, which reads every tripwire at every merge, asks whether the horizon has
passed as it asks whether the tripwire fired, per
`design@agent-skills@standing-state-review-before-every-merge`. The exits are one text, the fragment
`path@agent-skills@fragments/tripwire-lifecycle.md`: it fires; its horizon passes, the deleting
commit naming the occasions the decision held through and a `rejected` entry that the tripwire's
doubt kept judged again, its removal the owner's; its decision is reversed; a condition of the
standard excludes it; another entry guards the same premise; or a firing's repair adds a check that
makes the failure loud.

The owner asked for a retirement clause, since a tripwire watches only a decision's failure and so
nothing observed ever retires one. The registers showed it: 250 tripwires added here and in thaum
since their start, 194 standing, none retired, one of 194 stating when it stopped being needed. The
firings came early: the 9 entries of this repository removed on firing had fired between 0 and 34
days after they were written. Two rivals lose: a count of occasions survived, kept in the entry,
which is churn at every occasion and wrong once a session forgets it; and an age or a version
sunset, which no evidence sets a period for, against `design@agent-skills@audit-triggers`, and which
fires on whoever crosses the date. A goal stays while it is met, per
`design@agent-skills@goal-lifecycle`, because it is the owner's standing intent; a tripwire is a
hypothesis about one premise, and the premise stays written in the head every grounding reads.

A firing's repair that adds a check leaves the tripwire, since the check then reports the failure,
which condition 1 of `design@agent-skills@tripwire-admission-test` excludes: thaum restated entries
on such repairs, 10 of its 17 firings found anyway. An exit for a tripwire whose evidence names an
artifact the project's instructions prevent is not kept: the surveys found it used 0 times in 250
entries, and a failure a test or a check reaches is excluded by condition 1.
