# Texts that state what a relocation costs are read again once its bound is argued

## Summary

Several heads and one issue state what a relocation of a project's documents costs, or an exception to it. The bound
they measure against is argued by no head, per `issue@check@the-cost-of-a-relocation-is-bounded-by-no-argued-head`.
Each is read again once that discussion argues the bound.

## Details

### What

The texts are those that entry lists as its instances, among them `path@check@docs/design/paths-and-links.md`,
`path@model@docs/design/anchors.md` and
`issue@model@a-reference-to-an-anchor-s-own-directory-breaks-when-the-anchor-moves`.

Two heads of `check` state an exception to a relocation that edits no document:

- `design@check@goals-home-cites-upward-only`, whose last sentence says a move that changes which Components enclose
  a goals home turns its citations of the former enclosing Component into findings, each repaired by hand;
- `design@check@every-path-names-its-anchor`, in `path@check@docs/design/paths-and-links.md`, whose exception "save
  a goals home whose enclosing Components the move changes" qualifies the claim that a relocation edits no document.

### Why it matters

A design head that states an exception to a bound binds sessions as a presumption, per `primer@intent-and-claims`,
and the exception cannot be judged while the bound it departs from is unargued. It strains
`design@check@every-path-names-its-anchor`.

### What would close it

Each of those texts, the two heads above included, read against the bound as
`issue@check@the-cost-of-a-relocation-is-bounded-by-no-argued-head` leaves it, and rewritten where it states a
measure or an exception the argued bound removes, under `skill@knowledge-architect-decision-recording` for a design
head.
