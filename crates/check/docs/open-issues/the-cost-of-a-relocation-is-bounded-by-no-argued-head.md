# No head argues that a relocation of a project's documents must cost mechanical edits alone, nor defines a relocation

## Summary

Two heads hold the mechanism by which a relocation of part of a project's documents edits one line of the manifest:
`design@check@every-path-names-its-anchor` and `design@model@register-home-reference`. No head argues the premise
they serve, that a relocation must cost no manual work beyond mechanical edits. No head defines a relocation, exempts
an adoption from it, or says when a project relocates.

## Details

### What

The statements nobody has argued, each with what it bound:

- "A project relocates part of its documents when it moves a Component, or another directory that holds its entries,
  moves the place where a kind of entry lives, or turns that place from one file into a directory." It defined a
  relocation. `design@check@every-path-names-its-anchor` speaks of relocating an anchor, and
  `design@model@register-home-reference` of a home that outgrows one file; no head holds a move of the place where a
  kind of entry lives.
- "A project relocates only on a change of purpose of the part it moves, which the goals carry and which justifies
  the move, or where the relocation, with mechanical edits alone, breaks no check." It bound when a session relocates.
  No installed text holds it, and its first occasion names a Component's goals, which no Component states.
- "Either way, changing the goals that the change of purpose calls for is the only manual work it needs, beside a few
  mechanical edits, such as a new name replaced wherever the old one is cited." It bound the cost of a relocation,
  and the two heads above took their requirement from it.
- "An existing project that brings its documents into conformance when it adopts the workflow makes no relocation
  under this goal.", with its case "Admits, for instance: an existing project that moves its decision records into
  the home the workflow gives them, when it adopts the workflow." It exempted an adoption.
- "the installed text has a session relocate part of a project's documents only on those two occasions." It bound
  the installed text.
- "Rules out, for instance: a Component moved for tidiness, with no change of purpose, after which a session must
  rewrite references one by one, by judgement, for the check to pass." It ruled out a move that leaves references
  to repair by judgement.

The instances seen, each a text that states what a relocation costs with no argument for why it must cost that:
`design@check@every-path-names-its-anchor`, `design@check@goals-home-cites-upward-only`,
`design@model@anchors-are-components-and-locations`, `design@model@register-home-reference`,
`design@model@path-to-a-home-is-reported`, `design@model@harness-kinds-cited-without-anchor` and
`design@model@declarable-directory-register`.

The suspected mechanism: the bound was held as a statement no argument had to defend, so the heads cited it and
argued the mechanism alone.

What an argument for the bound has to answer. A derivation exists from
`goal@knowledge-architect@records-reach-their-reader`: a recorded item is cited where it bears, so a citation that
does not survive a move of what it names reaches nobody until it is repaired. Its rival is a relocation repaired
reference by reference, from the list of dangling references the checker gives. No fact found defeats that rival: it
weighs a count of edits, which no goal ranks.

### Why it matters

A session that weighs a change against the mechanism, such as a spelling that a move breaks, has no argued bound to
weigh it with: `issue@model@a-reference-to-an-anchor-s-own-directory-breaks-when-the-anchor-moves` is such a case.
It strains `design@check@every-path-names-its-anchor` and `design@model@register-home-reference`, whose rivals lose
on a cost that nothing ranks.

### What would close it

A design discussion under `skill@knowledge-architect-design`, raised when a change is weighed against the cost of a
relocation, or on the owner's word. It argues the bound, with the definition of a relocation and the exemption of an
adoption, as statements of the two heads or as a head of its own, and decides whether any text tells a session when
to relocate. Where no fact ranks a relocation at mechanical edits alone over one repaired reference by reference,
the choice is a fork for the owner.
