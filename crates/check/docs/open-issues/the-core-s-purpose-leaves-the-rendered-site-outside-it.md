# `check`'s goals home states a purpose that leaves its goal on a rendered site outside it

## Summary

`goal@knowledge-architect@projects-are-split-into-components` asks that each Component holding Rust
code in its own files serve one purpose, which the head of its goals home states. Every head now
states one, on the owner's ruling in the review of the whole goal set. `check`'s head says the
checker "exists mostly for" `goal@knowledge-architect@agents-work-without-drift`, which leaves
`goal@knowledge-architect@documents-render-as-a-linked-site` outside it, and no root goal now names a rendered
site.

## Details

### What

A head of `check` whose purpose covers the rendered site, or the site's goal placed in a Component whose
purpose covers it, or a design head of `check` that argues the second purpose; each written under
`skill@knowledge-architect-goal-setting`.

### Why it matters

Until `check`'s purpose covers every goal of its home, the one-purpose test cannot be judged for
`check`, and the site's goal reads as a second purpose.

### What would close it

`check`'s head, or the site goal's placement, ruled by the owner so that `check`'s goals serve
one stated purpose; or a design head of `check` that argues the departure.
