# Whether two heads of the query design home each bundle a second decision

## Summary

Two readings, each raised by a design-record auditor at the check of step 0 of the record search
workflow's spec and left unsettled there, say that a head of `register@query@design` holds a second
decision: the removal paragraph of `design@query@occasion-shaped-output`, and the "and" of the
title of `design@query@worklist-writes-and-verifies-the-reports`. Both heads are older than that
work, which did not settle either reading, since a split would need an argument it did not hold.

## Details

### What

- `design@query@occasion-shaped-output`: its paragraph "A run removes these files of a previous run
  before it judges anything, and a write that fails removes those written before it, so a run that
  fails ... leaves none of them" may be a decision of its own, losing to the rival of a run that
  leaves a previous run's outputs, or writes part of its own. The other reading, which the
  design-record auditor that checked the review repairs before the merge took, is that the
  paragraph delivers the title's "a passing `worklist --verify` writes", so it belongs to the head.
- `design@query@worklist-writes-and-verifies-the-reports`: its title joins what `worklist --out`
  writes and what `worklist --verify` judges by "and". The writer and the judge may answer two
  questions with two nearest rivals, or one question, that no searcher derives its group or writes
  its report by hand, as the title's last clause states.

### Why it matters

A head that bundles two decisions is cited for one of them by a text that a change to the other
leaves false, and a reversal of one leaves the other's argument mixed with its successor's, against
`goal@design-is-recorded-with-its-arguments`.

### What would close it

Each reading judged under `skill@knowledge-architect-decision-recording@head-shape`, the test of one
decision per head: each statement given its nearest rival. Where two statements lose to different
rivals, the head is split, and the split argued under `skill@knowledge-architect-design` where it
needs an argument the head does not hold; otherwise this entry closes with the reason.
