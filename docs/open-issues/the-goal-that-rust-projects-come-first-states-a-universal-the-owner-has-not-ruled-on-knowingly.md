# The goal that Rust projects come first states a universal that no ruling shows the owner chose knowingly

## Summary

`goal@knowledge-architect@rust-projects-come-first` says a Rust project gets "every check and tool
the project offers". The goal standard that `spec@plans@goal-standard-and-review` plans asks, in its
rule 3, that a goal rule out nothing the owner has not ruled out knowingly. Did the owner choose that
universal, or does the goal reach wider than the owner's intent?

## Details

### What

Found in the design discussion on goals, which put the question to the owner as Q4. It is recorded
as a question for the whole-set review of this repository's goals, the session's default the owner
accepted, rather than ruled on then; the owner then brought that review onto the spec's own branch.

### Why it matters

A goal binds outright, per `primer@goals-bind`, so a universal wider than the owner's intent rules
out designs the owner would have admitted, against `goal@knowledge-architect@the-owner-decides`, under which the owner
states the project's intent in its goals.

### What would close it

The whole-set review of this repository's goals that step 6 of that spec runs on its branch, on the
owner's word, whose finding on this goal the owner rules on under
`skill@knowledge-architect-goal-setting`: the goal kept as it stands, or reworded.
