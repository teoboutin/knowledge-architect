# The heads a harvest will touch, judged and conformed before the harvest

The third slice of `milestone@plans@split-guidelines-and-plan-directories`. When it lands, it is
moved under its milestone's `harvested/` and stays there until the milestone leaves, under the rule
slice 1 builds. The threads, arguments and decided design it shares with the other
slices are in the milestone document; what only this slice builds is below.

## Builds

- **The planning skill**:
  - a plan document lists, in its section "What is already decided", the heads its harvest will
    touch without reversing or rewriting them, beside the heads it reverses or rewrites;
  - at planning, once that list exists, one `agent@knowledge-architect-design-record-auditor` is
    dispatched on the listed heads, with a brief scoped to them, never a whole audit of the axis; its
    property is a point of view apart, per `design@agent-skills@work-divided-by-judgement`;
  - each head the auditor finds non-conformant gets a step that conforms it before the harvest: a
    step of its own, or a slice of its own where the conforming work is large; a conforming step
    that must write a missing argument, or the constraint a head takes from a goal, is a decision,
    and goes to the design skill or to the owner as `primer@design-heads` routes it;
  - each slice's design audit runs the scoped auditor again over the heads its slice's harvest
    touches, since the record moves between slices.
- **The record**: `design@agent-skills@existing-heads-on-touch`, rewritten, below, with every text
  that cites it.
- **CHANGELOG.md**: the entries the change owes, in the `Next release` section.

## Claims

1. **The plan lists the touched heads.** The installed planning skill has a plan document list the
   heads its harvest touches without reversing them, in its section "What is already decided".
2. **One scoped auditor, at planning.** The installed planning skill dispatches one
   `agent@knowledge-architect-design-record-auditor` on that list at planning, scoped to it, with
   its property named, and never the axis's saved workflow; the auditor's load stays under the group
   size of `design@agent-skills@design-record-axis-dispatch`, 60 entries by default, and a list above
   it is cut as that head cuts a group.
3. **A conforming step comes before the harvest.** The installed planning skill places each
   conforming step before the harvest, and routes one that owes a decision to the design skill or to
   the owner.
4. **The harvest conforms no unlisted head.** At this slice's own harvest, every head the harvest
   touches is in the list its spec holds, or the review reports it: the spec-conformity review's
   reading of P6.

## Decided design

For `thread@split-guidelines-and-plan-directories@pre-harvest-conformance`.

- **The list**: the heads the harvest touches without reversing or rewriting them, in the plan's
  section "What is already decided", beside the heads it reverses or rewrites.
- **The judgement**: one design-record auditor at planning, scoped to the list; a point of view apart,
  the property `design@agent-skills@work-divided-by-judgement` asks of a dispatch a step fixes.
- **The conforming step**: before the harvest, a step of its own or a slice of its own; where it must
  write what a head lacks, an argument or a goal's constraint, it owes the design skill or the owner,
  per `argument@split-guidelines-and-plan-directories@arg-26`.
- **The case stands on the record alone**: `issue@check@core-heads-bundle-decisions-with-many-citing-sites`,
  open since 2026-10-09, and `design@agent-skills@existing-heads-on-touch`, by which a touched head is
  brought to the rules in the change that touches it. The argument from review cost was dropped in
  round 3, `argument@split-guidelines-and-plan-directories@arg-46`.
- Nearest rival: conforming touched heads in the harvest, as today. Defeated by the owner's report,
  `argument@split-guidelines-and-plan-directories@arg-8`: the design-record review cannot judge a head
  the harvest changed without conforming it, and conforming it there brings unplanned work.
