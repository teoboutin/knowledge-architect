## Decided design

For `thread@split-guidelines-and-plan-directories@component-split-argues-the-criteria`.

- **The rule**: the head that argues a Component's existence, and the record of a Component that
  departs from a default criterion, reasons over each shipped criterion and the signals against a
  split. An illustration of the shape, not authority, from round 1:

  ```markdown
  - one purpose: met — <why it pays here>
  - independent reason to change: departed — <the argument, or the cost accepted>
  - signals against a split: <which ones were weighed, and why none prevails>
  ```

- **Where it is stated**: in step 3 of the component-creation skill, and judged by the decision-record
  reviewer; at two sites, so the instruction earns a head in `agent-skills`,
  `argument@split-guidelines-and-plan-directories@arg-22`.
- **Its first use**: the twelve root heads, `argument@split-guidelines-and-plan-directories@arg-43`.
- **Its delivery to adopting projects**: the Migration entry that cites the design-record axis.
- Nearest rival: no argument over the criteria required, today's text. Defeated by
  `goal@design-is-recorded-with-its-arguments`, and by the owner's
  `argument@split-guidelines-and-plan-directories@arg-3`.
